## Issues That Matter

These are the key issues I will fight for as your representative in Little Rock.

---

### Public Education

Our public schools are the foundation of our community's future. I will work to:

- Protect funding for public schools against voucher programs that drain resources
- Support teachers with competitive salaries and classroom resources
- Expand access to pre-K education for all Arkansas families
- Ensure rural schools receive equitable funding

---

### Food Security

Arkansas has been identified as the most food-insecure state in the nation. We must:

- Protect and expand programs like Meals on Wheels for seniors
- Support the Farm to School initiative connecting local farmers with school cafeterias
- Strengthen food banks and community pantries
- Ensure all children have access to free school meals

---

### Healthcare Access

Healthcare should be affordable and accessible for all Arkansans:

- Push for caps on healthcare and insurance costs
- Protect rural hospitals and clinics from closure
- Expand access to mental health services
- Support programs that help seniors afford prescription medications

---

### Foster Care Reform

Our most vulnerable children deserve better:

- Increase funding for foster care services
- Improve training and support for foster families
- Reduce caseloads for social workers
- Create better pathways to permanent homes for children in care

---

### Supporting Local Agriculture

Our farmers are the backbone of Arkansas:

- Protect independent farmers' markets
- Support programs connecting local producers with schools and institutions
- Advocate for fair prices and sustainable farming practices

---

### Senior Services

Our seniors built this community and deserve our support:

- Protect Medicare and Social Security
- Expand Meals on Wheels and home care services
- Ensure affordable housing options for seniors
- Support community centers and programs that reduce isolation
