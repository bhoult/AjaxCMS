## Why I Am Running

The recent catastrophic cuts to federal funding sources have shaken many organizations in our community. Critical programs that feed and provide services for our most vulnerable citizens have been gutted without warning, and many people working with these organizations have lost their jobs. The Arkansas LEARNS Act is a state-level implementation that has a few benefits, but the impact of taking tax dollars away from our public school system will be absolutely devastating to our public schools. We need to re-structure our community in a way that will strengthen our local relationships while decreasing our dependency on federal monies. We must build partnerships with state-level and local organizations that can help us mitigate the damage from these extreme and unexpected cuts to critical resources.

Arkansas has been identified as the most food-insecure state in the entire nation. I applaud our Governor, Sarah Huckabee Sanders, for passing legislation to feed all public school children for free. I think the citizens of Russellville would also benefit greatly from the protection of funding for our public schools, and by having legislatively-imposed caps on outrageous healthcare and insurance costs. We also need an improved and better-funded foster care system. Our independent farmers' markets, shelters for people in need, and programs for seniors (like Meals on Wheels) also need support and protection. Some of these important organizations are complimentary to each other and could be streamlined to work together to the benefit of everyone, such as the Farm to School program that was, unfortunately, recently canceled.

I would like to serve in a state-level leadership position where I can help identify and acknowledge these vital programs, introduce legislation to streamline and strengthen organizations in our community, and protect the jobs of people who work in these needed areas.
