## Community Resources

Here are helpful resources for residents of the River Valley region.

---

### Food Assistance

**River Valley Regional Food Bank**
- Phone: (479) 785-0582
- Provides emergency food assistance to families in need

**Meals on Wheels - Pope County**
- Delivers nutritious meals to homebound seniors
- Contact your local Area Agency on Aging

**SNAP Benefits (Food Stamps)**
- Apply online at [access.arkansas.gov](https://access.arkansas.gov)
- Or visit your local DHS office

---

### Healthcare Resources

**ARKids First**
- Health insurance for children
- Apply at [access.arkansas.gov](https://access.arkansas.gov)

**Community Health Centers of Arkansas**
- Affordable healthcare on a sliding fee scale
- Multiple locations throughout the River Valley

**Arkansas 211**
- Dial 2-1-1 for help finding local services
- Available 24/7

---

### Education Resources

**Russellville School District**
- [russellvilleschools.net](http://russellvilleschools.net)

**Arkansas Tech University**
- [atu.edu](http://atu.edu)

**Arkansas Scholarship Lottery**
- College scholarships for Arkansas students
- [myarkansaslottery.com](http://myarkansaslottery.com)

---

### Senior Services

**Area Agency on Aging of Western Arkansas**
- Phone: (479) 783-4500
- Services include Meals on Wheels, transportation, and caregiver support

**Social Security Administration**
- [ssa.gov](http://ssa.gov)
- Call 1-800-772-1213

---

### Voter Information

**Arkansas Secretary of State - Voter Services**
- Check your registration at [voterview.ar-sos.gov](https://voterview.ar-sos.gov)
- Find your polling location
- Request an absentee ballot

**Pope County Election Commission**
- Phone: (479) 968-7509
- Located in the Pope County Courthouse
