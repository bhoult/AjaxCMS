Jennifer Lewter is a lifelong Arkansan and dedicated public servant running for state office to represent the citizens of Russellville and the River Valley region.

### Background

Jennifer has deep roots in the Arkansas community. With years of experience working alongside local organizations, she understands the challenges facing working families, students, and seniors in our region.

### Education

- Master's Degree in Biology
- Certified in Community Development Leadership

### Professional Experience

- 15+ years working with community organizations
- Advocate for public education funding
- Volunteer coordinator for local food banks and senior services

### Family

Jennifer is proud to call Russellville home. She is committed to building a stronger community for the next generation of Arkansans.

### Vision

"I believe that good government means listening to the people it serves. My door will always be open to the citizens of Arkansas."
