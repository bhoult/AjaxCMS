## Network Connections Theme

Visualize **digital connectivity** with an animated network of nodes and connections. Watch as data flows through an ever-evolving web of relationships.

### About This Theme

In our connected world, networks are everywhere - social graphs, neural networks, distributed systems, and human relationships. This theme visualizes that interconnectedness with nodes that connect, disconnect, and form new patterns.

**Connected Visualization:**
- **Dynamic Nodes** - Points of connection move and interact
- **Flowing Connections** - Lines form and break as nodes move
- **Network Intelligence** - Connections form based on proximity
- **Tech Aesthetic** - Modern, digital visual language

### Network Theory in Motion

Each node follows simple rules - move randomly and connect to nearby neighbors. From these simple rules emerges complex network behavior, demonstrating principles of graph theory and distributed systems.

### The Connected Age

From the internet to social media to blockchain, networks define modern technology and society. This theme celebrates that interconnectedness, making abstract network concepts visually tangible.
