## Organic Design

Growth is a minimalist theme that embraces organic movement. As you navigate through the pages, watch as plants grow and evolve around your content, creating a unique experience with every visit.

### Features

- **Adaptive Growth**: Plants detect your content and grow around it naturally
- **Dynamic Animation**: Vines and trees continuously grow and branch
- **Smooth Transitions**: When pages change, plants crumble and regrow
- **Minimal Design**: Clean black, white, and gray color scheme
- **Borderless Content**: Content flows freely without visual constraints

The background responds to your content, creating a living canvas that changes as you explore.
