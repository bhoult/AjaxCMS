# Philosophy

## Growth as a Metaphor

Like plants in nature, ideas and content should have room to breathe and grow. This theme embodies the philosophy that design should be organic, not constrained by rigid boxes and borders.

### Natural Movement

The animated plants aren't just decoration - they represent the living nature of content. Each page transition is like a season change, where old growth fades away and new life emerges.

### Minimalism

By stripping away unnecessary visual clutter, we create space for content to shine. The borderless design allows your words and ideas to flow naturally across the page.

### Adaptation

Just as plants adapt to their environment, this theme adapts to your content. The growth patterns change based on the layout and structure of each page, creating a unique visual experience every time.

## Design Principles

1. **Simplicity**: Clean, minimal aesthetics
2. **Motion**: Continuous, organic animation
3. **Adaptation**: Responsive to content changes
4. **Nature**: Inspired by plant growth patterns
5. **Flow**: Borderless, seamless transitions
