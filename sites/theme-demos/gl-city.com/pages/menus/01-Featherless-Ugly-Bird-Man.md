## The Night the UFOs Came

It was a quiet night in GL-City. The usual hum of traffic had faded to a whisper, and the only sounds were the gentle rustle of clouds drifting across the moon-lit sky. Citizens slept peacefully in their high-rise apartments, unaware of the threat that lurked beyond the atmosphere.

### The Invasion Begins

Without warning, they appeared. Metallic saucers emerged from behind the clouds, their colored lights flashing in hypnotic patterns as they descended over the city. Their spotlight beams swept across rooftops and streets, searching. The UFOs weren't here for peace or observation—they were hunting.

One by one, citizens began to vanish. The alien spotlight beams would lock onto buildings, and when they retracted moments later, entire families had disappeared without a trace. Panic spread through GL-City as people realized they were under attack.

The police were helpless. Their bullets ricocheted harmlessly off the alien craft. The military scrambled jets, but the UFOs moved with impossible agility, weaving through the city's skyscrapers at angles that defied physics. Their depth-shifting flight patterns made them nearly impossible to track.

### The Signal

High atop the GCPD headquarters, Commissioner Gordon made a desperate decision. His hand trembling, he activated the Featherless Ugly Bird Signal. The powerful searchlight pierced the night sky, its iconic silhouette—a plucked, awkward-looking bird—a beacon of hope against the chaos.

From the shadows, the Featherless Ugly Bird Man emerged. His cape (made from recycled feather dusters) billowed awkwardly as he surveyed the scene from a rooftop water tower. One of the dusters was still partially attached to its wooden handle, which poked out at an unfortunate angle. Below, another UFO swept its beam across a residential building. He had minutes at most.

He struck what he believed to be an intimidating pose, but the water tower creaked ominously under his weight. He quickly adjusted his stance.

### The Featherless Vigilante's Plan

The Featherless Ugly Bird Man had faced many threats, but never from beyond the stars. Yet as he watched the UFOs hunt, he noticed a pattern. The craft moved in waves—closer and farther, left to right—following predictable paths as they scanned the city. Their spotlight beams were powerful, but they were also a vulnerability. Each time a beam activated, the UFO had to slow down and hover.

He contacted Alfred through his modified chicken-shaped communicator. "I need you to redirect all available power from the east grid to sector seven. Every light, every billboard, every possible source."

"Sir, that will leave thousands without power," Alfred replied, concern evident in his voice. "Also, sir, are you certain about the costume redesign? The featherless look is quite... bold. And dare I say, somewhat poultry-esque?"

"They're already being taken, Alfred. We don't have a choice. And the feathers were slowing me down during gliding maneuvers."

"Ah yes, the gliding. Sir, might I remind you that chickens don't actually—"

"Not now, Alfred!"

### The Trap

The Featherless Ugly Bird Man leaped from rooftop to rooftop with an ungainly flapping motion, placing modified EMP devices on the tallest buildings in sector seven. Each leap was accompanied by a *whoosh-thwap* sound as his cape awkwardly caught the wind. Each device was calibrated to emit a specific electromagnetic frequency—one that would interfere with the UFOs' navigation systems, but only when they were at their most vulnerable.

On the third rooftop, he misjudged a landing and had to scramble up from a decorative chimney. "Still got it," he muttered, dusting himself off.

As he worked, a shooting star streaked across the sky. Or was it? The Featherless Ugly Bird Man realized the "shooting stars" were actually escape pods—victims being transported to the mothership. Time was running out.

He activated the Featherless Ugly Bird Signal himself, repositioning it to point directly at sector seven. The awkward bird silhouette wobbled in the beam—at this angle, it looked less like a menacing bird of prey and more like a confused rubber chicken.

"It's... it's very distinctive, sir," Alfred offered diplomatically through the communicator.

Then he activated Alfred's power surge.

The entire sector blazed to life—a beacon of light impossible for the UFOs to ignore. Just as the Featherless Ugly Bird Man predicted, three craft converged on the area, their spotlight beams activating as they descended to investigate.

### The Strike

"Now!" the Featherless Ugly Bird Man commanded in his gravelly, slightly squawky voice.

The EMP devices fired simultaneously. The electromagnetic pulse wasn't strong enough to destroy the craft, but it disrupted their antigravity systems for crucial seconds. The UFOs wobbled, their smooth flight patterns breaking into erratic spirals.

The Featherless Ugly Bird Man fired his grappling hook (shaped like a bird talon) at the nearest craft. The line's tip—equipped with his patented Dingle-Berry Adhesion Device™—made contact with the metallic hull with a satisfying *splort* sound. The sticky purple sphere adhered firmly to the UFO's surface, dangling slightly as the craft wobbled.

"The Dingle-Berry holds!" he announced triumphantly into his communicator.

"Sir, I really must insist we workshop a better name for that gadget," Alfred's voice crackled back.

As the UFO struggled to regain control, he swung directly into its spotlight beam, the Dingle-Berry device stretching but maintaining its grip.

But he wasn't being abducted. His suit's modified plating (which he'd borrowed from a disco ball at a condemned nightclub) reflected the beam back at the UFO itself. The alien technology, designed to capture and transport, couldn't handle its own energy signature being reversed. The craft's systems overloaded in a cascade of sparking lights.

"That's right!" he squawked victoriously. "You just got bird-slapped by science!"

One down. The Dingle-Berry device released with a satisfied *plop* as the UFO spiraled away.

### The Retreat

The other UFOs, witnessing their companion's fate, immediately broke off their attack patterns. They rose rapidly into the sky, their lights flickering as they retreated. The Featherless Ugly Bird Man had shown them that humanity (even the featherless and aesthetically challenged among them) wouldn't go quietly into the night.

As dawn broke over GL-City, the Featherless Ugly Bird Signal still shone from its rooftop perch, its awkward silhouette somehow more inspiring than ever. Citizens emerged from their homes, looking up at the fading stars. The UFOs were gone, and though some had been taken, many more had been saved.

Commissioner Gordon found the Featherless Ugly Bird Man standing atop GCPD headquarters, silhouetted against the rising sun—his featherless form looking particularly ungainly in the morning light.

"Will they come back?" Gordon asked.

The Featherless Ugly Bird Man watched the sky for a long moment. A cloud drifted past, catching the first rays of morning light. In the distance, a plane's contrail could be mistaken for a spacecraft.

"They'll come back," he replied in his gravelly, slightly squawky voice. "But now they know—this city is protected. As long as that signal can shine, as long as there are those willing to stand against the darkness—no matter how featherless or ugly they may be—GL-City will endure."

He vanished into the shadows with an awkward hop. Well, "vanished" was perhaps too strong a word—one of the feather duster handles caught briefly on a flagpole, causing him to dangle for a moment before unhooking himself with as much dignity as he could muster.

He left behind only the faint *click-click-click* sound of his talon-shaped grappling line retracting, a single purple Dingle-Berry stuck to a nearby ventilation shaft, and the promise that when the UFOs returned, the Featherless Ugly Bird Man would be ready.

But don't worry—somewhere in the darkness, the Featherless Ugly Bird Signal stands ready, and the Featherless Ugly Bird Man watches over GL-City.*
