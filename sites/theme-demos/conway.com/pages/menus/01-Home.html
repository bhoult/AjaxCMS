Welcome to Conway's Game of Life

This site showcases an interactive implementation of Conway's Game of Life as an animated background theme for AjaxCMS.

What You're Seeing

The animated background behind this content is Conway's Game of Life - a cellular automaton devised by mathematician John Horton Conway in 1970. Watch as cells come alive, survive, or die based on simple mathematical rules.

Features

  • Interactive Drawing: Click and drag on the background to add live cells to the simulation!
  • Real-time Animation: Each generation updates automatically based on the game's rules
  • Infinite Grid: The simulation wraps around the edges for continuous patterns
  • Random Initialization: Every page load generates a unique starting pattern
  • Matrix Theme: Green-on-black styling complements the mathematical nature of the simulation
  • Touch Support: Works on mobile devices - draw with your finger!

The Rules

Conway's Game of Life follows four simple rules:

  1. Underpopulation: Any live cell with fewer than 2 neighbors dies
  2. Survival: Any live cell with 2 or 3 neighbors survives to the next generation
  3. Overpopulation: Any live cell with more than 3 neighbors dies
  4. Reproduction: Any dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive

These simple rules create surprisingly complex and beautiful patterns that have fascinated mathematicians and computer scientists for decades.
