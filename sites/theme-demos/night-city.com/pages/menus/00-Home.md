## Night City Theme

Welcome to the Night City theme for AjaxCMS. This theme immerses your site in a procedurally generated nighttime metropolis with infinite parallax scrolling, twinkling stars, a glowing moon, and drifting clouds. Every building, mountain, and window light is generated by code, creating a unique cityscape that never repeats.

### About This Theme

The Night City theme brings the magic of an urban night sky to your AjaxCMS site with fully procedurally generated graphics. No image files are used—every building, mountain, star, cloud, and window light is drawn in real-time using HTML5 Canvas. The cityscape features five depth layers of buildings and three layers of mountains, all scrolling at different speeds to create authentic parallax depth. As you scroll down the page, the entire city rises smoothly, revealing more of the night sky.

Built with a dark modern aesthetic featuring deep navy backgrounds, golden amber accent lighting reminiscent of city lights at night, and clean contemporary typography, the Night City theme delivers a sophisticated urban atmosphere. Poppins font provides bold modern headings while Inter delivers crisp body text perfect for nighttime reading.

### Key Features

The Night City theme creates a living, breathing urban environment:

- **Procedurally Generated Buildings**: Hundreds of unique buildings with random heights, widths, and window patterns—no two buildings are identical
- **Flickering Window Lights**: Each window has individual brightness and flicker patterns, creating the illusion of life inside the city
- **Five Building Depth Layers**: Buildings scroll at different speeds based on their depth, creating realistic perspective
- **Three Mountain Layers**: Distant mountain silhouettes with atmospheric blue tinting for depth perception
- **150 Twinkling Stars**: Each star has its own size, brightness, and twinkle pattern scattered across the upper sky
- **Glowing Moon**: Positioned in the upper right with soft radial glow and subtle crater details
- **Drifting Clouds**: Two cloud layers moving at different speeds (slow distant clouds, faster near clouds)
- **Scroll-Based Parallax**: The entire cityscape rises as you scroll down, revealing more sky and stars
- **Modern Typography**: Poppins (bold, contemporary) for headings and Inter (clean, readable) for body text
- **Golden Amber Lighting**: Warm #FFC864 accents evoke glowing city lights and streetlamps
- **Glassmorphism Containers**: Semi-transparent panels with backdrop blur and glowing borders
- **Responsive Animation**: Entire scene regenerates on window resize, maintaining proportions

### Technical Excellence

Built for Bootstrap 5 compatibility, the Night City theme uses vanilla JavaScript with HTML5 Canvas 2D rendering for all graphics. Every element is drawn procedurally using mathematical functions—buildings are randomized rectangles with grid-based window placement, mountains use triangular peak generation, stars use circular arcs with sine-wave twinkling, and clouds are composite circular paths.

The parallax system calculates scroll offset individually for each depth layer, creating smooth multi-plane motion. Building positions wrap around the screen edge for infinite scrolling. Window lights use sine waves for natural flickering. The moon uses radial gradients for atmospheric glow. Typography leverages Google Fonts with Poppins providing contemporary sans-serif headings (weights 400-800) and Inter delivering modern body text (weights 400-600).

### Live Animation

The background continuously generates and animates a nighttime cityscape. Buildings scroll horizontally from right to left at speeds based on their depth (foreground buildings faster, background buildings slower). Mountains drift very slowly in the far background. Stars twinkle independently with individual patterns. Clouds drift across at two different speeds. Window lights flicker realistically. As you scroll down, the entire scene rises with parallax offsets, revealing more of the starry sky and creating dramatic vertical depth.

### Technical Implementation

**Building Generation**: Each building is an object with random width (40-120px), height (100-300px), and depth layer (0-4). Windows are generated in a grid pattern with 70% probability of being lit. Each lit window has random brightness (50-100%) and flicker phase for animation.

**Mountain Generation**: Each mountain layer generates 8-12 triangular peaks at random positions with varying heights and widths. Mountains have atmospheric blue tinting that increases with depth.

**Star Generation**: 150 stars are randomly positioned in the upper 60% of the screen. Each has random size (0.5-2px), brightness (30-100%), and twinkle speed (0.02-0.05).

**Cloud Generation**: 9 clouds total—4 in the slow layer, 5 in the fast layer. Each is composed of overlapping circles forming fluffy cloud shapes.

**Parallax Calculation**: Position offset = basePosition + (scrollOffset / depthLayer). Deeper layers divide scroll offset by larger numbers, moving slower. Buildings and mountains wrap horizontally when scrolling off-screen.

### Pure Code, No Images

Unlike traditional themes that use pre-rendered images, Night City generates everything in code. This means:
- **Zero image loading time** - instant display
- **Infinite variation** - every refresh generates new buildings
- **Perfect scaling** - crisp rendering at any resolution
- **Tiny file size** - just JavaScript, no large PNG files
- **Easy customization** - change colors and sizes in code

The entire cityscape is drawn in approximately 60 frames per second using requestAnimationFrame for smooth animation performance.
