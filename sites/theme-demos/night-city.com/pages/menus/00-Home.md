## Night City Theme

Welcome to the Night City theme for AjaxCMS. This theme immerses your site in a procedurally generated nighttime metropolis with infinite parallax scrolling, enhanced twinkling stars, shooting stars, occasional UFOs, a glowing moon, and drifting blurred clouds. Every building, mountain, rooftop structure, and window light is generated by code, creating a unique cityscape that never repeats.

### About This Theme

The Night City theme brings the magic of an urban night sky to your AjaxCMS site with fully procedurally generated graphics. No image files are used—every building, mountain, star, cloud, and window light is drawn in real-time using HTML5 Canvas. The cityscape features ten depth layers of buildings and three layers of mountains, all scrolling at different speeds to create authentic parallax depth. As you scroll down the page, the entire city rises smoothly, revealing more of the night sky.

Built with a dark modern aesthetic featuring deep navy backgrounds, golden amber accent lighting reminiscent of city lights at night, and clean contemporary typography, the Night City theme delivers a sophisticated urban atmosphere. Poppins font provides bold modern headings while Inter delivers crisp body text perfect for nighttime reading.

### Key Features

The Night City theme creates a living, breathing urban environment:

- **Procedurally Generated Buildings**: Hundreds of unique buildings with random heights, widths, and window patterns—no two buildings are identical
- **Rooftop Details**: Solar panels, water towers, billboards, HVAC units, and antennas add realistic urban architecture
- **Flickering Window Lights**: Each window has individual brightness and flicker patterns, with occasional on/off behavior
- **Ten Building Depth Layers**: Buildings scroll at different speeds based on their depth, creating dramatic perspective
- **Three Mountain Layers**: Distant mountain silhouettes with atmospheric blue tinting and increasing roughness for depth
- **150 Twinkling Stars**: Enhanced flickering effect with each star having its own size, brightness, and twinkle pattern
- **Shooting Stars**: Occasional meteors streak across the sky with variable tail lengths (30-240 pixels) and smooth fading
- **UFO Easter Egg**: Rare flying saucers (every 1-2 minutes) with wobbling motion, colorful lights, and tractor beams
- **Glowing Moon**: Large moon positioned in the upper left with soft radial glow and detailed crater patterns
- **Blurred Clouds**: Twelve pre-rendered cloud shapes in five varieties, moving at different speeds with realistic blur
- **Scroll-Based Parallax**: The entire cityscape rises as you scroll down, revealing more sky and stars
- **Modern Typography**: Poppins (bold, contemporary) for headings and Inter (clean, readable) for body text
- **Golden Amber Lighting**: Warm #FFC864 accents evoke glowing city lights and streetlamps
- **Glassmorphism Containers**: Semi-transparent panels with backdrop blur and glowing borders
- **Responsive Animation**: Entire scene regenerates on window resize, maintaining proportions

### Technical Excellence

Built for Bootstrap 5 compatibility, the Night City theme uses vanilla JavaScript with HTML5 Canvas 2D rendering for all graphics. Every element is drawn procedurally using mathematical functions—buildings are randomized rectangles with grid-based window placement, mountains use triangular peak generation, stars use circular arcs with sine-wave twinkling, and clouds are composite circular paths.

The parallax system calculates scroll offset individually for each depth layer, creating smooth multi-plane motion. Building positions wrap around the screen edge for infinite scrolling. Window lights use sine waves for natural flickering. The moon uses radial gradients for atmospheric glow. Typography leverages Google Fonts with Poppins providing contemporary sans-serif headings (weights 400-800) and Inter delivering modern body text (weights 400-600).

### Live Animation

The background continuously generates and animates a nighttime cityscape. Buildings scroll horizontally from right to left at speeds based on their depth (foreground buildings faster, background buildings slower). Rooftop structures add architectural detail to the skyline. Mountains drift very slowly in the far background. Stars twinkle independently with enhanced flickering, occasionally joined by shooting stars streaking across the upper sky. Watch for rare UFO sightings with their colorful blinking lights and wobbling flight paths! Clouds drift across at two different speeds with realistic blur effects. Window lights flicker realistically, with 3% occasionally turning on and off. As you scroll down, the entire scene rises with parallax offsets, revealing more of the starry sky and creating dramatic vertical depth.

### Technical Implementation

**Building Generation**: Each building is an object with random width (50-140px scaled to screen), height (150-450px scaled to screen), and depth layer (0-9). Buildings feature rooftop structures including solar panels (50%), water towers (40%), billboards (30%), HVAC units (50%), and antennas (40%). Roofs are slightly darker than building bodies for realistic architectural appearance.

**Window Animation**: Windows are generated in a grid pattern with 65% probability of being lit. Each lit window has random brightness (40-100%) and flicker phase for sine-wave animation. 3% of windows feature on/off behavior with random timing (on: 150-350 frames, off: 50-150 frames).

**Mountain Generation**: Three mountain layers with 8-12 triangular peaks each. Distant mountains have increased roughness with high-frequency sine waves and more noise (10-55px variation). Mountains have atmospheric blue tinting that increases with depth for realistic atmospheric perspective.

**Star Animation**: 150 stars with enhanced flickering (50-100% brightness variation) randomly positioned in the upper 60% of the screen. Shooting stars spawn every 3-6 seconds with variable tail lengths (30-240 pixels), diagonal trajectories, and smooth fade in/out over 60 frames.

**UFO Easter Egg**: UFOs spawn approximately every 1-2 minutes, flying left-to-right or right-to-left across the upper third of the sky. Features wobbling motion, five rotating colored lights (red, green, blue, yellow), occasional tractor beam effect, and metallic dome/saucer design with blue atmospheric glow.

**Cloud Generation**: 12 pre-rendered cloud shapes in 5 unique varieties with 8px blur applied once at initialization for performance. Clouds spawn at ±500px off-screen to prevent pop-in. Uses off-screen canvas rendering with drawImage() for GPU-accelerated display.

**Parallax Calculation**: Position offset = basePosition + (scrollOffset / depthLayer). Deeper layers divide scroll offset by larger numbers, moving slower. Buildings and mountains wrap horizontally when scrolling off-screen.

### Pure Code, No Images

Unlike traditional themes that use pre-rendered images, Night City generates everything in code. This means:
- **Zero image loading time** - instant display
- **Infinite variation** - every refresh generates new buildings
- **Perfect scaling** - crisp rendering at any resolution
- **Tiny file size** - just JavaScript, no large PNG files
- **Easy customization** - change colors and sizes in code

The entire cityscape is drawn in approximately 60 frames per second using requestAnimationFrame for smooth animation performance.
