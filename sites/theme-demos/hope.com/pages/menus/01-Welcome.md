## A Theme of Renewal

Hope is a theme about resilience and new beginnings. Watch as trees grow from nothing, reaching toward the sky. When the page changes, they fall away - but new growth always returns. Every ending is a new beginning.

### Features

- **Persistent Growth**: Trees continuously reach upward, seeking light
- **Autumn Leaves**: Leaves change color and drift gently to the ground
- **Renewal**: When pages change, the old fades and new life emerges
- **Sky Gradient**: A calming blue sky fades to white, symbolizing clarity
- **Natural Beauty**: Organic forms that feel alive and hopeful

The trees grow around your content, adapting to whatever you create. Life finds a way.
