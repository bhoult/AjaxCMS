# Philosophy

## Hope as a Metaphor

Trees have always symbolized hope. They grow slowly but steadily, weathering storms and seasons. They lose their leaves in autumn, yet always return in spring. This theme captures that eternal cycle of loss and renewal.

### The Cycle of Seasons

The animated trees aren't just decoration - they tell a story. Watch as leaves turn golden and red, then drift gently down. It's a reminder that endings are natural, and that something new always follows.

### Reaching Upward

Trees grow toward the light. No matter what obstacles they encounter, they find a way around them, always reaching higher. Your content becomes part of this landscape - the trees grow around it, incorporating it into their world.

### Letting Go

The falling leaves represent release. Sometimes we must let go of the old to make room for the new. When you navigate to a new page, the trees fade away - but only so that fresh growth can emerge.

## Core Themes

1. **Resilience**: Growth despite obstacles
2. **Renewal**: Every ending brings a new beginning
3. **Patience**: Beautiful things take time
4. **Adaptation**: Finding a way through challenges
5. **Light**: Always reaching toward something brighter
