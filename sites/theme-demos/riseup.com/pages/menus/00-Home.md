## Rise Up Theme

Ascend to new heights with **uplifting particles** that rise endlessly toward the sky. Experience the inspiring motion of continuous upward momentum.

### About This Theme

The Rise Up theme embodies aspiration, growth, and positive energy. Thousands of particles drift upward in a never-ending ascent, creating a visual metaphor for progress and achievement.

**Upward Motion:**
- **Continuous Ascent** - Particles rise from bottom to top in perpetual motion
- **Varying Speeds** - Different rise rates create depth and visual interest
- **Subtle Fading** - Particles gradually fade as they ascend
- **Inspiring Energy** - Upward movement creates positive psychological impact

### The Psychology of Upward Movement

Studies show that upward motion creates associations with growth, success, and positivity. This theme harnesses that psychological effect to create an inherently optimistic and motivating atmosphere.

### Visual Dynamics

Each particle follows its own path upward, with slight variations in speed and trajectory creating organic, natural movement. The effect is both calming and energizing - a paradox that makes it endlessly watchable.
