# Fireworks Theme

Welcome to the **Fireworks Theme** demo! This theme features a spectacular animated background with fireworks bursting across a night sky, complete with flying UFOs and interactive elements.

## Features

### Fireworks Display
- **Dynamic Fireworks Animation**: Watch as colorful fireworks launch and explode with realistic physics
- **Multiple Explosion Types**: Circular bursts, random patterns, and ring explosions
- **Streamer Effects**: Long-lasting particle trails that drift through the sky
- **Secondary Explosions**: Some fireworks create additional bursts for extra spectacle
- **Vibrant Color Schemes**: Red-orange-yellow, blue-cyan, pink-magenta, green, purple, and gold combinations
- **American Flag Pattern**: Special firework that creates red, white, and blue star patterns
- **Strobe Effects**: Pulsing particles that create dramatic lighting effects
- **Particle Control**: 1 in 3 fireworks creates larger, more dramatic explosions

### Interactive UFO Elements
- **Flying UFOs**: Watch for mysterious UFOs that fly across the sky every 18-36 seconds
- **Click to Destroy**: Click on any UFO to make it explode into pieces
- **Realistic Physics**: UFO parts (dome, body, lights) fly apart and fall with gravity and rotation
- **Animated Details**: UFOs feature rotating colored lights, wobbling motion, and occasional tractor beams
- **Large and Noticeable**: UFOs are prominently sized (60-105px) for easy spotting

### Visual Polish
- **Header Star Stream**: Animated stars flow down from the header in patriotic red, white, and blue
- **Frosted Glass Header**: Semi-transparent header with backdrop blur effect
- **Twinkling Background Stars**: Subtle stars that twinkle in the night sky
- **Performance Optimized**: Smooth 60fps animation with efficient rendering
- **Layered Canvas**: Foreground elements (UFOs) appear in front of content while allowing text selection

## Theme Design

The Fireworks theme uses a festive color palette perfect for celebrations:

- **Primary Colors**: Warm oranges (#ff6b35) and reds (#c73e1d)
- **Accent Colors**: Bright yellow (#ffd23f) and vibrant red (#ff4757)
- **Typography**: Poppins for body text and Righteous for headings
- **Dark Background**: Deep navy (#1a1a2e) allows the fireworks to shine
