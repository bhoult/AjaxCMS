# Fireworks Theme

Welcome to the **Fireworks Theme** demo! This theme features a spectacular animated background with fireworks bursting across a night sky.

## Features

- **Dynamic Fireworks Animation**: Watch as colorful fireworks launch and explode with realistic physics
- **Multiple Explosion Types**: Circular bursts, random patterns, and ring explosions
- **Streamer Effects**: Long-lasting particle trails that drift through the sky
- **Secondary Explosions**: Some fireworks create additional bursts for extra spectacle
- **Vibrant Color Schemes**: Red-orange-yellow, blue-cyan, pink-magenta, green, purple, and gold combinations

## Theme Design

The Fireworks theme uses a festive color palette perfect for celebrations:

- **Primary Colors**: Warm oranges (#ff6b35) and reds (#c73e1d)
- **Accent Colors**: Bright yellow (#ffd23f) and vibrant red (#ff4757)
- **Typography**: Poppins for body text and Righteous for headings
- **Dark Background**: Deep navy (#1a1a2e) allows the fireworks to shine

## Perfect For

This theme is ideal for:

- Celebration and event websites
- Festival announcements
- New Year's sites
- Party planning services
- Any project that needs a festive, energetic vibe

Enjoy the show!
