### The Mathematics Behind the Beauty

Plasma effects combine multiple sine waves with different frequencies, phases, and amplitudes. Each pixel's color is determined by:

```
color = sin(x * freq1 + time) + sin(y * freq2 + time) + sin(distance * freq3)
```

This simple formula creates infinitely complex, ever-shifting patterns that have captivated computer artists for decades.

### Demoscene Heritage

The demoscene emerged in the 1980s as crackers added artistic intro screens to pirated software. It evolved into a legitimate art form where programmers, musicians, and artists compete to create the most impressive real-time audiovisual experiences within strict technical constraints.

Plasma effects became a signature element of demos, showcasing what skilled programmers could achieve with limited hardware.
